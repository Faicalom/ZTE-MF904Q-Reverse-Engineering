# Contributing

Keep historical observations separate from new hypotheses. Cite the source filename, relevant function or line range, hardware/build and exact hash for every proposed procedural change. Label host simulation, on-device measurement and owner observation separately.

Proposals for new hardware require a separate compatibility investigation. Do not broaden support because an SoC or vendor matches. Do not submit factory firmware or private dumps. Reproduce an issue with sanitized small transcripts or a synthetic fixture and clearly label the fixture. Prefer a minimal patch with local tests. Never convert a missing historical command into a “standard MediaTek” command.

Licensing is pending owner and component review. Do not assume that possession of a file grants redistribution rights. Identify your contribution's authorship and proposed terms before a public merge. No CLA or contributor license policy has been adopted.
