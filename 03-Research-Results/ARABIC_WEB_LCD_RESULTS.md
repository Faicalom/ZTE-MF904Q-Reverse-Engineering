# Arabic localization: visible success and permanent instability

Temporary Arabic/English WebUI output and Arabic LCD resources were demonstrated in the historical project. The owner confirmed the Arabic hotspot label and later whole Arabic LCD interface during permanent-image boots. These are positive localization results and must not be erased by calling every trial a total localization failure.

Permanent combined WEB+LCD, WEB_ONLY and owner-reported LCD_ONLY boots nevertheless powered off. The user later also described off/on behavior; shutdown versus reset was not causally resolved by a final continuous trace. Full Android boot evidence exists in earlier captures, but appearance of Arabic alone never proved all Android services or cellular Internet remained healthy.
